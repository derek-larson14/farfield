# What Farfield reads

The default sources. During setup, and any time after, you can add your own or mute any of these. [Suggest a source](https://github.com/derek-larson14/farfield/issues/new?template=source.yml)

## Builders and engineering

- **[Anthropic Engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering)**: Concrete harness, evaluation, and tool-use implementation lessons.
- **[Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog)**: Open model tooling, reproducible implementations, datasets, and practical experiments.
- **[Hamel Husain](https://hamel.dev/)**: Task-specific evaluations and failure analysis from real AI projects.
- **[Martin Alderson](https://martinalderson.com/)**: Firsthand work on agent speed, costs, infrastructure, and software economics.
- **[OpenAI Engineering](https://openai.com/news/engineering/)**: How OpenAI builds and runs its systems: harnesses, infrastructure, and lessons from deployment.
- **[Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/)**: Production ML, retrieval, evaluation, and deployment tradeoffs.
- **[Every](https://every.to/)**: Workflows, creative practice, and organizational adoption from people building with AI.
- **[Chip Huyen](https://huyenchip.com/)**: Systematic explanations of AI systems and product tradeoffs.
- **[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)**: Daily hands-on notes on LLM tools, prompt injection and agents, with links to primary sources.
- **[Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/)**: Long interviews and essays with people building AI products and infrastructure.
- **[Hacker News (front page)](https://news.ycombinator.com/)**: Front-page Hacker News stories above 150 points: a broad net for originals outside the list.

## Research

- **[Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research)**: Primary experiments in model behavior, safety, and economic use.
- **[OpenAI Research](https://openai.com/news/research/)**: Primary research and system cards from OpenAI. Vendor research; read it next to independent evaluations.
- **[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/)**: Primary work in models, robotics, and scientific applications.
- **[Interconnects](https://www.interconnects.ai/)**: Nathan Lambert on post-training, open models and RL, from someone doing the work.
- **[Ahead of AI (Sebastian Raschka)](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)**: Technical explanations connecting papers to model training and evaluation.
- **[Berkeley AI Research](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/)**: Researchers explain their own work in learning, robotics, and systems.
- **[Lilian Weng](https://lilianweng.github.io/)**: Deep technical surveys linking methods, papers, and limitations.
- **[Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/)**: Jack Clark's weekly digest of notable papers, with his own reading of each.
- **[Dwarkesh](https://www.dwarkesh.com/)**: Long interviews with researchers and builders, with full transcripts.

## Evaluation and adoption

- **[METR](https://metr.org/)**: Independent evaluations of agent capabilities and effects on real work.
- **[AI as Normal Technology](https://www.normaltech.ai/)**: Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor on evaluation quality, adoption, and limits of AI claims.
- **[Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/)**: Measurement of compute, capabilities, inference, and economic adoption.
- **[One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/)**: Ethan Mollick on how people actually use AI at work, often citing new studies.
- **[Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news)**: Research on adoption, labor, institutions, and the social effects of AI.

## Business and strategy

- **[Benedict Evans](https://www.ben-evans.com/)**: Technology adoption and business-model analysis beyond individual model launches.
- **[Derek Thompson](https://www.derekthompson.org/)**: Big-picture essays on AI, the economy and how people actually adopt new technology.

## Outside AI

- **[Construction Physics](https://www.construction-physics.com/)**: Mechanisms of technological diffusion, production, and cost improvement outside software.
- **[Works in Progress](https://worksinprogress.co/)**: Detailed accounts of scientific, institutional, and industrial progress.

## Papers

- **[arXiv targeted searches](https://arxiv.org/)**: Narrow, dated searches built from your open questions, so new papers surface without popularity rankings.
- **[Hugging Face daily papers](https://huggingface.co/papers)**: A daily shortlist of papers the community found interesting.

## Podcasts

- **[No Priors](https://www.nopriors.com/)**: Sarah Guo and Elad Gil interview the founders and researchers building AI companies.
- **[Lenny’s Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast)**: Product decisions, growth and firsthand accounts of adopting AI.
- **[How I AI with Claire Vo](https://www.youtube.com/@howiaipodcast)**: Demonstrations of how people actually use AI at work.
- **[The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/podcast)**: Engineering practice, developer productivity and organizational adoption.
- **[The Every Podcast (formerly AI & I)](https://every.to/podcast)**: Conversations and demonstrations about AI and how people work.
- **[Odd Lots](https://www.bloomberg.com/oddlots)**: Bloomberg's markets podcast; AI economics, energy and compute from outside tech.
- **[The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/)**: Long technical interviews with founders and researchers.

## Not on the list yet

Worth trying, not switched on:

- [smol.ai AI News](https://news.smol.ai/): broad AI and developer chatter; good for finding originals. Overlaps Latent Space.
- [Last Week in AI](https://lastweekin.ai/): weekly roundup, useful for discovery.
- [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch): an editor's weekly shortlist across applied AI and research.
- [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/): compute and chips; mostly paywalled.
- [Learning the Machine](https://davisblalock.substack.com/): selective technical reading; check how often it still publishes.
- TWiML, Software Engineering Daily and The Data Exchange: podcasts with patchier transcripts.
