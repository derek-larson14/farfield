## Other discovery routes worth trying

These are research candidates, not silently enabled sources:

- [smol.ai AI News](https://news.smol.ai/rss.xml): broad AI and developer discussion, including social chatter. Useful for finding original work; overlaps Latent Space.
- [Last Week in AI](https://rss.art19.com/last-week-in-ai): weekly episode notes and links for broad discovery.
- [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch): an editorial shortlist across applied AI and research.
- [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/feed): compute and semiconductor reporting; paywalls and feed freshness need checking.
- [Learning the Machine](https://davisblalock.substack.com/feed): selective technical reading; evaluate its current cadence.

TWiML, Software Engineering Daily and The Data Exchange are further podcast candidates. Transcript coverage is less consistent, so they are not defaults. A roundup and the paper it links to count as one evidence chain.
